# STAR Method

## Situation
Set the scene briefly. Name the project or context, the relevant constraint, and why the situation mattered. Give enough detail for a listener unfamiliar with the work, but avoid a long background story.

## Task
Explain your responsibility and the outcome you were expected to deliver. Separate what the team owned from what you personally owned so the interviewer can understand your scope.

## Action
Describe the specific steps you took, in first person. Explain the reasoning behind important choices and how you worked with others. Spend the most time here; a list of team activities does not show your individual contribution.

## Result
Close with the outcome and evidence: a metric, a delivered capability, feedback, or a lesson. If the result was mixed, say what changed and what you would do differently next time.
