# Handling Failure

## Owning What Happened
Choose a real setback and describe your own part in it plainly. Explain the impact without blaming teammates or hiding behind vague phrases such as "things went wrong."

## Responding and Recovering
Describe the immediate action you took to reduce impact, communicate status, and help restore progress. Be clear about what you did versus what the wider team did.

## Learning and Prevention
Explain what you learned and what concrete behavior or process changed afterward. A useful lesson is specific and shows how you reduced the chance or impact of a similar problem.
