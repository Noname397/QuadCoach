# Interview Communication

## Structuring an Answer
Lead with the main point, then give only the context needed to understand it. Organize the explanation into a small number of steps and make the connection between the problem, your action, and the outcome explicit.

## Making Your Contribution Clear
Use "I" for your own decisions and work, and "we" when describing shared outcomes. Define specialized terms when needed and avoid assuming the listener knows the details of your project.

## Checking Understanding
Pause at natural points and invite a follow-up. If a question is broad, briefly state how you are interpreting it before answering. If you do not know something, say what you do know and explain how you would investigate the rest.
