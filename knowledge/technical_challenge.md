# Technical Challenge

## Diagnosing the Problem
Explain the observable symptom and how you narrowed down its cause. Mention the evidence you checked, such as logs, a failing test, a metric, or a small reproduction. Avoid jumping from symptom straight to fix.

## Implementing a Fix
Describe the change you made and why it addressed the cause. Include important constraints, such as keeping an API compatible, handling partial failures, or protecting existing data.

## Verifying the Fix
Explain how you tested the change and how you checked for regressions. A strong account distinguishes the test that reproduced the bug from the broader checks that built confidence in the fix.
