# Project Tradeoffs

## Explaining a Decision
State the problem and the requirements that shaped the decision before naming the technology. Connect each requirement to the choice you made rather than presenting a tool name as the whole explanation.

## Comparing Alternatives
Mention one realistic alternative and the tradeoff it would have introduced. Consider cost, complexity, performance, reliability, delivery time, and the team's familiarity. Explain why the chosen option was a better fit for this situation, not universally better.

## Revisiting the Choice
Describe evidence that would make you reconsider the decision, such as changed scale, a new reliability target, or operational costs. Showing a willingness to revisit a choice demonstrates judgment rather than defensiveness.
